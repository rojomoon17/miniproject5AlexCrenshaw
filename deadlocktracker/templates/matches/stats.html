How your ratings are worked out

1. How much each game counts

Games from the {{ full_weight_days }} days before your latest game count as full games; after that a game counts half as much every {{ half_life_days }} days. The "Counts as" column in your match history shows each game's weight.

2. Your average: {{ average['result'] }}%

  • Your record: {{ average['wins'] }}-{{ average['losses'] }} ({{ average['plain'] }}%).
  • Weighted by age it counts as {{ average['eff_wins'] }} wins in {{ average['eff_games'] }} games ({{ average['weighted'] }}%).
  • Pulled towards 50% by {{ baseline_prior_games }} games, which matters most while you're new:
    ({{ baseline_prior_games }} × 50% + {{ average['eff_wins'] }}) ÷ ({{ baseline_prior_games }} + {{ average['eff_games'] }}) ≈ {{ average['result'] }}%

3. Each hero's rating

Each hero starts at your average, counted as {{ prior }} games, and its own weighted games are added on top:

(starting games × your average + weighted wins) ÷ (starting games + weighted games)

The number of starting games ({{ k_min }}–{{ k_max }}) comes from your data: the more your heroes' records differ beyond what luck explains, the fewer it uses. "Rating built from" below shows how much of each rating is that hero's own games and how much is your average. More detail
