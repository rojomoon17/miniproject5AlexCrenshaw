Getting started
  1. What to fill in: your hero, win or loss, the lane you played (Yellow, Blue or Green) and the other 11 heroes in the game. Mark your lane partner and your two lane opponents. Souls, length (minutes:seconds), K/D/A, side and notes are optional.
  2. What you'll see after one game: your match history, stats, and a page for that hero where you can keep notes.
  3. As you keep playing: a lane matchup, partner or teammate appears on a hero's best/toughest lists once you've played with or against it {{ min_list_games }} times. A hero joins the rankings after {{ min_ranked_games }} games.
  4. Ratings start at an even 50% and only move away from it as your record builds, so a single lucky win won't top the charts. Recent games count more. The full explanation is on the Rankings page.
