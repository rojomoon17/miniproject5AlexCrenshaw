Ratings are win rates adjusted for how many games there are and how recent they are (games from the {{ full_weight_days }} days before your latest count fully, then half as much every {{ half_life_days }} days). Each starts from {{ baseline_label }}, {{ baseline }}%{% if baseline_note %} ({{ baseline_note }}){% endif %}, shown as the yellow line on the bars, and only moves away from it as the games add up.
