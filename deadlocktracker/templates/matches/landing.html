How it works
  1. Log each game after you play it. Matchups are kept separate per hero, so beating someone in lane on one hero doesn't count for another.
  2. Everything starts at an even 50% and moves as your games add up. One win won't make a hero look like your best; it takes a real record to move the numbers far.
  3. More shows up as you play. A hero gets its own page after your first game on it, a matchup is listed once you've seen that hero {{ min_list_games }} times, and a hero is ranked after {{ min_ranked_games }} games.
  4. Recent games count more. A game {{ half_life_days }} days older than your latest counts half as much, so the numbers follow how you play now.
