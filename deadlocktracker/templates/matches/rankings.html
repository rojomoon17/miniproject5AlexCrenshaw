How the ranking works

A plain win rate is misleading on small samples (win your only game on a hero and it reads 100%) and treats a game from months ago the same as last night's. Each rating fixes both:

  1. Recent games count more. A game {{ half_life_days }} days old counts half as much as one today, and one {{ half_life_days * 2 }} days old a quarter, so ratings follow how you play now and the current patch. W-L records and game counts still show every game.
  2. Every rating starts from your average. Each hero starts as if it already had some games at your overall win rate ({{ baseline }}% across {{ total_games }} games), and your real games are added on top. How many "starting games" to use is worked out from your own data: the more your heroes' win rates differ by more than luck would explain, the fewer starting games, so real differences show through. For your heroes right now that's {{ prior }} games.
  3. Every rating comes with a range. The bar shows where your true win rate probably is ({{ range_percent }}% likely). With your numbers, a 1-0 record rates {{ examples['one_win']['rating'] }}% ({{ examples['one_win']['low']|round|int }}–{{ examples['one_win']['high']|round|int }}%), 6-1 rates {{ examples['six_one']['rating'] }}% ({{ examples['six_one']['low']|round|int }}–{{ examples['six_one']['high']|round|int }}%) and 20-10 rates {{ examples['thirty']['rating'] }}% ({{ examples['thirty']['low']|round|int }}–{{ examples['thirty']['high']|round|int }}%): more games, narrower range.
  4. Only heroes with {{ min_ranked_games }}+ games are ranked. Heroes with fewer games are listed under "Not enough games yet". Without this, a hero with one or two games ("probably about average") would often outrank a main that's proven a bit below average, which isn't what a ranking of your best heroes should do.

Lanes and matchups on a hero page work the same way, starting from your win rate on that hero. The best ones are ordered by the low end of their range and the toughest by the high end, so the ones we're surest about come first, and a hero needs at least {{ min_list_games }} games with or against you to be listed.
