How the ranking works

A plain win rate jumps around on small samples: win your only game on a hero and it shows 100%. Two things stop that from deciding your rankings:

  1. Only heroes with {{ min_ranked_games }}+ games are ranked. Heroes with fewer games are listed under "Not enough games yet" instead.
  2. Ranked heroes are ordered by rating. The rating imagines every hero already has {{ hero_prior_games }} games at your overall win rate ({{ baseline }}% across {{ total_games }} games), then adds your real games on top, so a longer record counts for more:

    rating = (wins + {{ hero_prior_games }} × {{ baseline }}%) ÷ (games + {{ hero_prior_games }})

    For example, a 1-0 record would rate {{ examples['one_win'] }}% and a 6-1 record {{ examples['six_one'] }}%.

Lanes and matchups on a hero page use the same rating, starting from your win rate on that hero but with a heavier weight of {{ matchup_prior_games }} games, because each matchup only has a few games and lucky streaks would otherwise crowd out real patterns. A hero also needs at least {{ min_list_games }} games with or against you to appear in a "best" or "toughest" list.
